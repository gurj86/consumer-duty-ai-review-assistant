# Public sources and interpretation

Checked 16 September 2026. Sources provide a framework; they do not prescribe the fictional case conclusions or an automatic compensation formula.

1. [FCA: About the Consumer Duty](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/consumer-duty/about). Basis for considering products and services, price and value, consumer understanding and consumer support; alongside good faith, foreseeable harm and support for financial objectives.
2. [FCA: Fair treatment of vulnerable customers, FG21/1 summary](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/finalised-guidance/guidance-firms-fair-treatment-vulnerable-customers). Basis for understanding individual support needs, adapting services and evaluating outcomes. The page’s July 2026 update directs readers to current Consumer Duty material for management-information expectations.
3. [FCA: Consumer Duty implementation — good practice and areas for improvement](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/good-and-poor-practice/consumer-duty-implementation-good-practice-and-areas-improvement). Supports attention to evidence of outcomes and the action taken when issues emerge.

Treating customers fairly is a broader conduct concept; the Consumer Duty should not be reduced to a generic fairness checklist. A service error does not, by itself, establish that every outcome has failed. Each mapping in this project is a question or provisional concern requiring evidence.

Case facts, dates, amounts, evidence records, priorities, reviewer roles and closure criteria were invented for this demonstration. They are not extracts from employer documents. The sample makes no claim about any actual firm's conduct.
